How it works

The Pomodoro technique: focus on one task for 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute break. After 4 focus sessions, take a longer 15-minute break.

Only finished focus sessions count in your stats. Your day streak is the number of days in a row with at least one session.

The timer keeps correct time even if you switch tabs or lock your phone for a while.

Everything is saved on this device only. No login, nothing sent anywhere.
